# Lisbon artwork

`lisbon.webp` is a decorative illustration generated with the built-in OpenAI
imagegen tool on 23 September 2026. It is not a photograph or geographic map.

Prompt: Create an elegant travel editorial illustration of Lisbon, Portugal
viewed across layered terracotta rooftops toward the wide blue Tagus river.
Warm sunlit cream and peach buildings, a few cypress trees, subtle azulejo-blue
details, soft sky. Gouache and cut-paper texture with restrained detail,
sophisticated travel journal aesthetic. Wide landscape composition roughly
3:1, no text, letters, borders, watermark, or people in the foreground. Focus
buildings toward the right half, quieter warm sky on the upper left.

The generated PNG was resized to 1800×600 and encoded as WebP for the app.
